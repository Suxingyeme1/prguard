# v0.13.2 phase report: independent review budgets and compatibility evidence

Status: implemented 2026-09-05.

## Objective

Turn the FileLock false accept into a reusable review mechanism without exposing or encoding its
sealed evaluator, and separate the resource budgets of repository implementation and independent
review.

## Product changes

- `IssueToPRTask` now carries `review_max_tool_calls`, independently of the Implementer's
  `max_tool_calls`; defaults are 12 and 24 respectively.
- `fix --review --review-max-tool-calls` and standalone `review --max-tool-calls` expose the
  distinction without changing existing task-file compatibility.
- Independent Review prepares two detached worktrees: an unchanged Base and the exact Candidate.
- A bounded Python AST comparison emits public signature/default, class-base, protocol behavior,
  implementation, and literal export-surface signals for changed non-test modules.
- Signals are supplied to the read-only Reviewer and frozen in JSON/Markdown Review artifacts.

## Verification

- unit coverage checks default/signature, protocol-method, export, private-symbol, test-path, and
  file-size boundaries;
- provider contracts verify compatibility evidence is present while Implementer reasoning, hidden
  labels, Gold Patch, and write tools remain absent;
- pipeline integration verifies Implementer and Reviewer budgets remain distinct through the CLI
  and Issue-to-PR runner;
- Review integration verifies signals survive recursive Artifact/Manifest generation;
- complete unit, integration, contract, and security suite: **263 passed**;
- Ruff lint: passed.

## Boundary and next priority

Static signals are review attention cues, not proof of a defect and not an automatic block. They do
not cover dynamic dispatch, non-Python APIs, generated interfaces, or unchanged files that matter
indirectly. FileLock #606 is contaminated after evaluator disclosure and will not be reused to claim
improved model quality. The next evidence step is a fresh compatibility-focused holdout, followed
by broader Python-version and repository-policy validation.
